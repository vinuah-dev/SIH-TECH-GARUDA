Camera positions

 

Pick a camera, then click the map to put it there — or paste its coordinates (in Google Maps, right-click the spot and click the numbers to copy them). Shift-click to point it. Drag to move around, scroll to zoom. Saved into the fleet file (config/cameras.json) and shown at once — no restart.

Virtual fences

Click on the picture to drop the corners of a fence, then name it and save. Coordinates are stored as fractions of the frame, so a fence keeps working if the camera resolution changes. A saved fence applies immediately — no restart.
